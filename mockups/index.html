LOL Bench — three redesign concepts

Three full-page mockups, all using real content: real jokes from the 1,699 matchups, real model names and anchor tiers, real harness stamps. Scores are illustrative while the pipeline drains. Each has a working vote booth — press A, B or T after it loads.

v2 — consolidates your feedback + the reference sites

The Instrument

One page, real data. Dense sortable leaderboard (± CI in every row), real per-model spend column and a “humor per dollar” scatter ($6.37 total, ARC-style), track tabs, live judging-coverage bars, and the vote booth. All numbers on this one are from your repo — no placeholders.

 concept a

The Preprint

A research paper about jokes, taken deadly seriously. arXiv meets The Onion — serif title block, red rubber stamp, academic whisker CIs, and handwritten margin notes. Light theme; flips the “every leaderboard is a dark dashboard” backdrop.

 concept b

The Sunday Funnies / Sports Desk

A newsprint front page: masthead with print misregistration, letters to the editor (the origin story), W–L–T box-score standings, comic-strip panels, and “Tonight’s Card” as the vote booth.

 concept c

The Control Room

Broadcast scoreboard: status bar with live clock, a joke ticker, LED-scoreboard leaderboard with family readouts, wave telemetry gauges, and a split-screen bout. The highest-craft, riskiest register — owned dark mode, not SaaS dark mode.

Shared across all three: real jokes front and center, the origin story promoted, designed “in-progress” states (per-model judging progress, ballot counters, LOL-C as a hook instead of a dead card), the error-bar whisker as recurring glyph, tabular numerals, keyboard voting, focus rings, and reduced-motion support. Mock data is labeled as such in each footer.
