LOL Bench

Can AI make you laugh? Measured in three faculties: appreciation, production, taste. Credibility-first.
PRELIMINARY - auto-judged, no human calibration yet. Every score below ships with n and a 95% CI. Judge model family is disjoint from scored candidates.

LOL-A - Appreciation auto-judged

150 items across six families: mechanism explanation, pun disambiguation, benign-violation reasoning, subtle inference, cultural reference, and failed-humor diagnosis ("why is this NOT funny"). Models must explain the joke; a disjoint-family judge grades explanations against expert gold elements.

Scores populate after the first overnight run.

LOL-B - Production humans vote

Every model writes under the same constrained premises. You vote on anonymous pairs. Win rates with Wilson confidence intervals appear as votes accumulate. No Elo until the vote volume justifies it - that is the honesty policy.

Voting opens when wave-0 outputs are published.

LOL-C - Taste automatic

Does a model's internal sense of humor match ours? Rank-correlation against human-rated joke pools, plus the self-awareness probe: does a model's taste track its own output quality?

Pending data import (graded joke pools).

Running tonight (wave 0)

Model lineup: GLM 5.3 Flash and DeepSeek V4 Flash (via B.AI). Every model answers the same 150 comprehension items and writes jokes for the same 40 constrained premises. Judging: DeepSeek grades GLM's explanations against gold rubrics; DeepSeek's own rows are held out of the leaderboard until a third judge family joins (no model grades its own family - that's the whole credibility policy).

Why the banner matters

Every prior attempt at humor evaluation died of judge mistrust, not topic disinterest. Research finding: human inter-annotator agreement on funniness caps around alpha ~0.45 - humans barely agree with each other. So LOL Bench publishes its own error bars first: judge-validity scores, human agreement, confidence intervals, on the leaderboard itself, not in an appendix. Claims ship when earned. Visibility ships now.
