Do AI models
get the joke?

LOL Bench measures three separate faculties: appreciation (explaining humor), production (writing it), and taste (matching human judgment). Every score ships with its error bars. Funniness is judged by humans, never by other models.

Why trust a number here

Every prior attempt at humor evaluation died of judge mistrust, not topic disinterest. So the protocol is built around the failure modes:

Failure modeDefense
One model grading its own familyDual judges, each barred from its own family; self-rows dropped defensively at scoring
Judge opinions on “funniness”Judges only check explanations against human-written gold elements — never judge funniness
Memorized benchmarksCanaries, private split, fresh premises each wave
OverclaimingPreliminary banner until human calibration exists; CIs on everything
